# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in the IUB PAY V1 project a
welcoming, respectful, and inclusive experience for everyone, regardless of
background, identity, experience level, or role.

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to:

- Treat others with respect and empathy.
- Communicate clearly and constructively.
- Welcome different viewpoints and experience levels.
- Give and accept feedback professionally.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing personal or confidential information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or unwanted advances.
- Trolling, insulting, or deliberately disruptive comments.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Threats or other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional
  educational or open-source setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and
pull requests that violate this code of conduct. They may also temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainers through the
repository owner or course/project supervisor. Do not use a public issue for
sensitive reports. Include enough context to support a fair review, but do not
include passwords, tokens, personal data, or other confidential information.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Retaliation against
anyone who makes a good-faith report is not acceptable.
